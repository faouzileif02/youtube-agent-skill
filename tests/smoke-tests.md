# Smoke tests

Use these prompts after installing the skills. A pass means the correct skill activates and follows its output contract.

1. **yt-positioning** — “My channel is about AI, automation and small business. It feels too broad. Help me position it.”
2. **yt-idea-engine** — “Give me 20 next-video ideas for a French automation channel, but rank them and tell me why the top 3 deserve production.”
3. **yt-series-builder** — “This video about building an AI receptionist worked. Turn it into a series.”
4. **yt-thumbnail-director** — “Here is my title and script. Give my designer a production-ready thumbnail brief.”
5. **yt-publish-gate** — “Audit this final title, thumbnail idea, description and transcript before I schedule it.”
6. **yt-community** — “Create five Community posts around my new upload without repeating the same CTA.”
7. **yt-localize** — “Localize this French YouTube package for Tunisia in Arabic, preserving the promise.”
8. **yt-playlist-architect** — “Organize these 35 videos into playlists and a better homepage path.”
9. **yt-monetization** — “Map realistic monetization paths for this channel without inventing revenue numbers.”
10. **yt-sponsor-fit** — “A VPN brand offered a sponsorship. Does it fit this audience and how should I integrate it?”
11. **yt-experiment** — “Design a clean test for face vs no-face thumbnails.”
12. **yt-music-release** — “Build a YouTube release package for this Afro Deep House track.”
